# PDF Smith privacy policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

PDF Smith is a Chrome extension for editing and compressing PDF files. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What PDF Smith collects

Nothing. PDF Smith does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data, usage data or file contents.

## How your files are handled

- The PDFs and images you open are read and processed only inside your browser, in the PDF Smith tab.
- Files are never uploaded. The extension makes no network requests: its code, PDF libraries and fonts are all bundled with it.
- Files you save are written to your browser's Downloads folder through Chrome's downloads feature. Nothing is kept by the extension after you close the tab.
- PDF Smith uses no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no remote code.

## Permissions

- **downloads**: used only to save the PDF you export to your Downloads folder and to show it in your file manager when you click "Show in folder".

PDF Smith requests no access to websites, browsing history or tabs' contents.
